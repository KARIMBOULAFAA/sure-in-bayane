About Sure In Bayane

An independent online community where medical students, doctors and researchers share their work, search the medical literature and find people to collaborate with.

Who we are

Sure In Bayane (SIB) was created for one simple reason: good research is easier to do together. Many students and young researchers work alone, far from people who study the same topics. SIB brings them into one place, so they can share their work, learn from each other and build projects together.

Our mission

To make medical research more open, more connected and easier to access, starting with students and researchers in our region and reaching anyone who wants to learn and contribute.

How it works

  1. Create a free account with your name and email.
  2. Complete your profile so others can find you, and choose whether to appear in the Researchers directory.
  3. Search and share: explore studies, or submit your own work for review.
  4. Connect: reach researchers from your university or city and start working together.

Who it is for

Medical students, interns and residents, doctors, researchers, professors and other health professionals, from any university or country.

Medical disclaimer

Everything on this site is for information and education only. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with a qualified health professional. Studies shared by members are reviewed only for basic suitability, they are not peer reviewed. Search results from PubMed, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Crossref and ClinicalTrials.gov come from third parties.

Community guidelines

Share only your own work, or work you have the right to share, and credit all co-authors. Never post anything that could identify a patient. No advertising or spam. We may remove content that breaks these rules.

Privacy

We store the name and email you register with, the profile details you choose to add (role, study level, degrees, university, faculty, country, city, specialty, ORCID, bio, photo) and the research you submit. Your name, profile details and photo are visible to visitors on your published research and your profile page. Your email is not shown on the site. If you switch on "Show me in the Researchers directory", other signed-in members can find you by university, city or specialty. You can switch it off at any time. Accounts are handled by Supabase. You can delete your submitted research from your profile at any time.
