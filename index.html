About Sure In Bayane

Sure In Bayane (SIB) is an independent community where medical students, doctors and researchers share their work, search the medical literature and get to know each other.

Medical disclaimer

Everything on this site is for information and education only. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with a qualified health professional. Studies shared by members are reviewed only for basic suitability, they are not peer reviewed. Search results from PubMed and OpenAlex come from third parties.

Community guidelines

Share only your own work, or work you have the right to share, and credit all co-authors. Never post anything that could identify a patient. No advertising or spam. We may remove content that breaks these rules.

Privacy

We store the name and email you register with, the profile details you choose to add (role, university, faculty, specialty, ORCID, bio, photo) and the research you submit. Your name, profile details and photo are visible to visitors on your published research and your profile page. Your email is not shown on the site. Accounts are handled by Supabase. You can delete your submitted research from your profile at any time.
